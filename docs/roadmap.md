# PlantMR release roadmap

## v1.0.0 — final scoped product

Complete summary-data MR, optional LD clumping, environment stratification, diagnostics, reports, tests, Conda and Docker packaging.

## v1.1 research extensions

- versioned SMR/HEIDI and GSMR adapters;
- colocalization adapter with explicit input/output contracts;
- multiple-testing correction and evidence tiers;
- simulation benchmarks with controlled weak instruments and pleiotropy.

## v2 research methods

- hierarchical environment-aware MR rather than independent stratification;
- polyploid dosage and homoeolog-aware instruments;
- PAV/SV and pan-genome coordinate contracts;
- independent real-crop benchmark and functional validation registry.

No v1.0 report should imply that these research extensions are already implemented.
